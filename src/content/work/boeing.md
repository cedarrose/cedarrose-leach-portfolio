The Boeing Satellite Development Center in El Segundo, California is a massive hub of achievement and inspiration. During my summer internship, I was immersed in the heart of aerospace innovation, touring the labs and warehouses that send such complex systems to space. I was surrounded by people of passion, expertise and grit, and I tried to take every opportunity to absorb their knowledge.

## FPGA project

My individual project was configuring a field-programmable gate array (FPGA) on the Versal VCK190 board: designing, simulating, synthesizing and implementing FPGA designs with Xilinx's Vivado and Vitis tools. It introduced me to the fundamentals of FPGA programming, which I've since built on by strengthening my Verilog skills in digital circuits courses at USC. I also studied GPS satellites, flight missions and the space environment.

## LINC Innovation Challenge

I also took part in Boeing's LINC Innovation Challenge, where my team developed a concept for an interactive campus navigation system for Boeing employees and visitors. We advanced through three competitive rounds and won. The judges were Boeing's Director of Human Resources and the President of Commercial Satellite Systems.

## Boeing USC Freshman Design Challenge

While on the topic of Boeing: as freshmen at USC, my friends and I entered Boeing's Freshman Design Challenge, where we optimized the cost, drag, lift and speed of a Boeing airplane by selecting its subsystem components. After pitching our configuration, we won the challenge and took home a model of the Boeing 737.
