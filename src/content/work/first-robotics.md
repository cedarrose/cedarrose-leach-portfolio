I was a member of FIRST Robotics Team 2637 through high school and served as its lead technical mentor.

I established and mentored an inaugural all-girls FIRST LEGO League robotics team for elementary school students. The team earned the Core Values Award, which recognizes gracious professionalism, and advanced to the Southern California State Championship.
